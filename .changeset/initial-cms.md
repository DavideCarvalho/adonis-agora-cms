First public release of the CMS: config-first schema with relational child tables, DDL diff/sync,
Local API, REST, access control, hooks, row and database tenancy, versions, drafts with autosave,
scheduled publishing, webhooks, plugins, i18n and the visual admin. Integrations are ports that take
an Agora adapter, an official AdonisJS one, or a custom object.
